Core ideas in plain language

Compound growth

Returns can generate additional returns. Time is often the biggest multiplier when contributions stay consistent.

Risk and return

Higher expected returns usually come with more ups and downs. Past performance does not promise future results.

Diversification

Owning a mix of assets can soften the blow when one holding struggles, though it does not remove risk entirely.

Inflation awareness

Cash that earns less than inflation slowly loses buying power. Comparing real (after-inflation) outcomes matters.
